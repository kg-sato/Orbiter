# Orbiter offline iPhone beta — privacy policy draft

Effective for the prepared 0.5.3 offline iPhone build, 2026-10-21.

**Publication prerequisite:** the owner must add their real support contact and publish this reviewed policy at a public URL before Apple submission. This local draft is not a published policy. It describes the offline iPhone build only; Windows and hosted-browser accounts require separate disclosures.

## Your information stays local

Orbiter's offline iPhone beta stores your schedule, tasks, course notes, focus history and preferences on your device. It does not require an account or send this information to the developer. This build has no advertising, analytics, tracking or automatic cloud synchronization.

The app uses Apple's persistent web storage for planner information and app-only preferences for local reminder bookkeeping. iOS backups may include app data according to your device settings. Removing the app or its data can remove your saved workspace. Export a separate JSON backup before uninstalling or replacing data.

## Files you choose to transfer

You can export JSON backups and calendar snapshots using Apple's Files interface. You choose where the files go, including any cloud or third-party provider you have enabled. Those providers handle the files under their own settings and policies. Orbiter does not send exports to the developer.

Import reads the file you select. A valid workspace import replaces the local workspace after validation; an invalid import preserves it. Keep a backup before importing. Calendar exports are snapshots, not a live connection to your calendar.

## Optional notifications and haptics

With your permission, Orbiter schedules local focus-completion reminders on this iPhone. These are not remote push notifications. Notification permission can be changed in iPhone Settings. Haptics provide optional local feedback.

## Your choices

Use the planner without signing in, export a copy of your workspace, edit or remove planner entries, and control optional reminders. No developer-held account exists for this offline build. Deleting the app can remove device-local data; copies in your exports or device backups remain under your control and the relevant provider's settings.

## Changes and questions

This policy must be reviewed before enabling account features, network services, analytics or other collection in a future iPhone build. The published policy must provide the developer's real support contact; no contact address is invented in this draft.
