The companion and its discoveries share a softer, moonlit-storybook character.

- Refined the original kitten's proportions, sculpted muzzle, inset eyes, ear bowls and matte materials while preserving course identities, growth, scarves and charms. Editable Blender source and SVG fallback are included.

Study data, native timer/reward authority, inventory IDs, app identity and disabled iPhone capability gates are preserved. The independent Obsidian research vault remains local; repository guidance links to its location.

## Validation and delivery

Discovery previews, equipment/fallback, chest animation/renderer restoration, avatar caching, cloud enrollment recovery, generation and iPhone source checks cover the changed paths with fictional data. Installer/signature verification is recorded separately after packaging. This Windows installer does not deliver an iPhone app.

Before installing manually, choose **Quit Orbiter** from the tray; × leaves it running. Installation, physical-device behavior, live service reconciliation and whole-process resource use remain separate checks. Updater signing is distinct from Windows Authenticode. A local installer/update manifest and an open PR do not publish the stable update feed.
