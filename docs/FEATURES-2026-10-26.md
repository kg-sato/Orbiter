## Seven-day habit tracker

Open **Life**, enable the local journal if desired, and add a ritual. Each habit now has seven date buttons ending on the selected **Journal date**. Select a day to record or unmark completion; the existing selected-day quick toggle remains. Pressed buttons show recorded dates; an unmarked date means not recorded. The range and full date are available to assistive technology.

The tracker uses existing `habits[].completions` and `M.toggleHabit`, without a schema or storage-key change. Saves use the existing owner, native-consent and persistence boundaries. Unsaved form entries, busy/read-only native storage, rejected writes and dates outside the visible range are guarded. Updating an earlier date does not retroactively mint journey lights or companion rewards; earned journey entries retain their existing semantics.
