# Orbiter 0.4 visual direction

## Intent

Ethereal and sculpted. Expressive scenery remains active while focusing; calmer motion and device accessibility preferences take priority. Keep study information still, readable, and actionable.

The reference reel is a collection of visual styles rather than an application specification. Use its bento, editorial, clay, and atmospheric ideas to create original art and a coherent student workspace.

## Research applied

- [Reference reel](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DdMUB4kskYv/): asymmetric composition, tangible objects, generous visual hierarchy, and environmental identity.
- [Apple: Meet Liquid Glass](https://developer.apple.com/videos/play/wwdc2025/219/): a separate floating plane for navigation/controls, material and motion designed together, clear contrast, and spatial continuity during morphs.
- [web.dev animation performance](https://web.dev/articles/animations-guide): transform/opacity motion, avoiding animated blur and layout, profiling paint, and sparing use of compositing layers.

## Scene language

Midnight canvas, mint light, lilac atmospheric depth, occasional warm opal glints. A few sculpted translucent ribbons, horizon pools, and distant particles replace repeated wave contours. Page compositions vary while deriving their tint from the selected accent.

My Day is an open horizon. Assignments uses ascending currents. Atlas adds floating habitats. My Rhythm uses measured horizontal bands. The buddy carries a miniature moonlit landscape. Original SVG/CSS artwork requires no extra WebGL contexts or downloaded stock assets.

## Motion and layout

Control feedback 160–220 ms; navigation/card motion 300–450 ms; major wipe approximately 700 ms. Background motion persists during focus. Pause hidden rendering, honor reduced motion, and keep pointer response bounded. One event-driven controller owns background input and visibility.

Existing data, timer authority, IDs, rewards, and export compatibility remain unchanged. Source changes stay uncommitted.
