# Exam preparation and orbital motion

Study items now distinguish Assignment/project from Test/exam. Exams keep one due date and a total study budget, preparation start date, and manual outside-timer logs. Completed linked Focus sessions plus manual logs reduce the remaining budget; starting a session or clicking Study does not claim work. Suggested daily effort divides the remaining minutes across the remaining days through the test date.

Upcoming tests is a persistent preparation section in Assignments. My Day shows those whose preparation period includes the selected day. No daily exam duplicates or calendar events are generated. When the budget reaches zero, the card stays available for review until the user marks the test finished. Completed items can reopen it. Manual minutes can be corrected in its editor; 366 dated records are retained. Metadata is optional for old backups and validated on import/account load.

The review popup has explicit insets for totals, chart, prompts and footer; the old large sculpture is removed to prevent layering behind the morph emblem.

The WebGL model now precesses around an inclined axis, gently nutates and rolls, and moves along a small three-dimensional elliptical path. Perspective size and surface lighting change with depth. It is an artistic orbit, not a physics simulation. Reduced motion draws a static pose.

Build/source review only; browser and device interaction review pending.

Calendar day selectors and week headers mark tests only on their actual test date. No repeated timed events are created.
