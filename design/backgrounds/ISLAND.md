# Companion floating diorama

Original geometry in src/mascot/cosmos.js creates a low recessed rear wall, inset atmospheric light, raised floor, luminous front lip and rounded floating underside. The cat sits in front of the rear wall; its head and ears exceed that silhouette and paws occupy the foreground. This uses actual depth and occlusion rather than a flat portrait backdrop.

All cats receive the basic stage. Owned habitats retain their IDs, props and palette; their environment is contained within the recess. Existing growth scenery and reward ownership remain unchanged. Bounded pointer attention gives slight differential wall motion. Reduced motion is static. The same renderer draws everything, and portraits include island version and scene in cache identity.

Reviewed neutral and equipped art renders plus desktop/phone compositions. No fresh native performance measurement or automated regression suite. The existing static failure fallback remains a flat portrait.
