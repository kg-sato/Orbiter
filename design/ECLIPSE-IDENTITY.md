# Eclipse identity

Original vector logo: web/icons/eclipse.svg. A dark circular horizon, an asymmetric accretion sweep and one guiding star. Palette: midnight, opal, violet-blue and warm starlight. Qi/cultivation influences appear as circulating energy and a calm dark centre, without borrowed symbols or literal characters.

The sidebar/mobile marks share the vector; the standalone and Apple launcher icons use the matching code-rendered eclipse. The front-page and Atlas sculpture now use a warped 3D accretion ribbon around a depth-tested dark sphere, with sparse celestial glints. This is a stylized black-hole-inspired sculpture, not a relativistic simulation. Reduced motion and offline fallbacks are preserved.

Atlas course cards now use individual vector eclipse seals instead of the legacy CSS planets. Course accent colors remain in the accretion gradients, orientation varies across cards, and the assignment completion ring and accessible course buttons remain functional.
